\# Model Card



\## Model Purpose



This project predicts systolic blood pressure using sleep health and lifestyle variables.



\## Intended Use



Educational and analytical machine learning workflow for exploring relationships between lifestyle factors and blood pressure.



\## Not Intended For



This model is not intended for clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions, or medical advice.



\## Inputs



\- Age

\- Gender

\- Occupation

\- Sleep Duration

\- Quality of Sleep

\- Physical Activity Level

\- Stress Level

\- BMI Category

\- Heart Rate

\- Daily Steps

\- Sleep Disorder



\## Output



Predicted systolic blood pressure.



\## Models Compared



\- Baseline Regressor

\- Random Forest

\- Gradient Boosting

\- SVR



\## Metrics



\- MAE

\- RMSE

\- R2



\## Limitations



The dataset is limited in size and may not generalize to broader populations.
